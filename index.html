Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what people usually ask before booking a painter.

What areas do you cover?

We cover Blenheim and about 100 km around it, across Marlborough: Renwick and the Wairau Valley, Picton and Queen Charlotte Drive, Havelock and Rai Valley, the road-accessible bays of the Marlborough Sounds, and south through Seddon to Ward. If you're not sure whether we cover your spot, give Scott a call and ask.

How do I get a quote?

Quotes are free and there's no obligation. Call Scott on 0210 298 3216 or send us an email with a few details about the job, and we'll sort a time to come and take a look.

What kinds of painting jobs do you take on?

Interior and exterior house painting, new builds and renovations, plus airless spraying, concrete floor coatings, fences, farm buildings and light commercial work.

Is the prep work included?

Yes. Prep is part of the job, including water blasting, plastering, sanding and masking, and we tidy up at the end of each day.

Are you qualified?

Yes. We're trade-qualified painters with more than 30 years on the tools.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes, our workmanship is guaranteed. If you'd like the details for your job, just ask Scott when he quotes.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 6pm. We're closed on Saturday and Sunday.
