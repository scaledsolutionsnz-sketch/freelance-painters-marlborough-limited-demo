Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Freelance Painters Marlborough Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This is placeholder wording for the purposes of this demonstration website and should be reviewed before going live.

1. What we collect

When you contact us for a quote or job, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and the details of the property and work you'd like done. We only collect what we need to quote and carry out your job.

2. How we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, schedule and complete work, invoice you, and keep in touch about your job. We do not sell your information to anyone.

3. Sharing

We may share limited details with trusted parties where needed to complete your job — for example a builder you're working with or a paint supplier. We only share what is necessary and expect the same care from them.

4. Storage & security

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet our legal and tax obligations, and take reasonable steps to keep it secure.

5. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request corrections. Contact us and we'll help.

6. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Call us on 0210 298 3216 or return to the homepage.
